
### Effort Calculation Formula

```
AI-ADJUSTED EFFORT CALCULATION
==============================

Traditional Effort = Base Hours × Complexity Factor × Risk Factor

AI-Assisted Effort = Traditional Effort ÷ AI Multiplier × Quality Factor

Where:
• AI Multiplier = Task-specific multiplier from table above
• Quality Factor = 1.1 (10% overhead for AI output review)

EXAMPLE CALCULATION
===================
Module: User Management (10 screens, medium complexity)

Traditional Estimate:
• Frontend: 80 hrs
• Backend: 60 hrs
• Testing: 40 hrs
• Documentation: 16 hrs
• Total: 196 hrs (4.9 weeks @ 40 hrs/week)

AI-Assisted Estimate:
• Frontend: 80 ÷ 3.0 × 1.1 = 29 hrs
• Backend: 60 ÷ 2.7 × 1.1 = 24 hrs
• Testing: 40 ÷ 4.0 × 1.1 = 11 hrs
• Documentation: 16 ÷ 4.0 × 1.1 = 4 hrs
• Total: 68 hrs (1.7 weeks @ 40 hrs/week)

Efficiency Gain: 65% reduction
```
