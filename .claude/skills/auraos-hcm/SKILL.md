# AuraOS HCM Skill

This skill provides domain expertise for the Human Capital Management module of AuraOS including:

## Core HCM Modules

- **Employee Management**: Employee profiles, onboarding, offboarding, document management
- **Payroll Processing**: Salary calculations, deductions, tax compliance, payslip generation
- **Attendance & Time**: Time tracking, shift management, overtime calculations
- **Leave Management**: Leave policies, accruals, approvals, holiday calendars
- **Recruitment**: Job postings, applicant tracking, interview scheduling, offer management
- **Performance Management**: Goal setting, reviews, 360-degree feedback, appraisals
- **Training & Development**: Learning management, certifications, skill tracking
- **Organizational Structure**: Departments, reporting hierarchies, cost centers

## When to Use

- Implementing new HCM features
- Designing HR workflows and approval processes
- Integrating with third-party HR tools
- Ensuring compliance with labor regulations
- Optimizing employee self-service portals
