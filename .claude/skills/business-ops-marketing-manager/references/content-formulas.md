## Full Post Frameworks

### AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)

**Structure:**

1. **Attention**: Hook that stops scrolling
2. **Interest**: Why this matters to them
3. **Desire**: Paint the picture of success
4. **Action**: Clear CTA

**Example:**

```
Your HR team is drowning in paperwork.       ← Attention

Every manual process costs 4x more than automation.
Every delayed onboarding loses you top talent.     ← Interest

Imagine: New hires productive on day 1.
Payroll running itself. Zero compliance errors.    ← Desire

That's what we built for Gulf Hotels Group.
Want to see how? Link in comments.              ← Action
```

### PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution)

**Structure:**

1. **Problem**: State the pain point
2. **Agitation**: Make it worse (consequences)
3. **Solution**: Offer the way out

**Example:**

```
Your asset management is a spreadsheet nightmare.      ← Problem

Every breakdown costs $10K+ in downtime.
Your team wastes 20 hours/week on manual tracking.
And you have no idea what assets you actually own.     ← Agitation

We built eAMS to fix exactly this.
Real-time tracking. Predictive maintenance.
ROI visible in 90 days.                                ← Solution
```

### BAB (Before, After, Bridge)

**Structure:**

1. **Before**: Current painful state
2. **After**: Desired future state
3. **Bridge**: How to get there

**Example:**

```
BEFORE: Scattered data across 5 systems.
Paper forms. Manual approvals. Lost documents.

AFTER: One platform. Automated workflows.
Real-time dashboards. Zero paperwork.

THE BRIDGE: We migrated a 500-employee hospital
to our system in 8 weeks.

Here's the exact process we used:
```

### 4Ps (Promise, Picture, Proof, Push)

**Structure:**

1. **Promise**: Bold claim/benefit
2. **Picture**: Help them visualize
3. **Proof**: Evidence it works
4. **Push**: CTA

**Example:**

```
Cut your recruitment time by 60%.                  ← Promise

Imagine posting a job Monday,
interviewing top candidates Wednesday,
and sending an offer by Friday.                    ← Picture

That's what ManithPro did for 3 enterprise clients
last quarter alone.                                ← Proof

Ready to transform your hiring?
Let's talk: link in bio                           ← Push
```

### The Story Arc

**Structure:**

1. **Setup**: Context and characters
2. **Conflict**: The challenge/problem
3. **Struggle**: Attempts and failures
4. **Resolution**: What worked
5. **Lesson**: Takeaway for reader

**Example:**

```
2 years ago, we pitched our biggest deal ever.
$2M contract. 18-month project.                    ← Setup

The client said no.
"Too expensive. Too risky. Too unknown."           ← Conflict

We went back 3 times.
Refined the proposal. Cut scope. Added guarantees.
Still no.                                          ← Struggle

Then we changed our approach entirely.
Instead of selling features, we sold outcomes.
We showed them exactly what success looked like.
They signed.                                       ← Resolution

The lesson?
Stop selling what you do.
Start selling what they get.                       ← Lesson
```

## Content Type Templates

### Case Study Post

```
[Client type] was struggling with [specific problem].

The symptoms:
→ [Pain point 1 with metric]
→ [Pain point 2 with metric]
→ [Pain point 3 with metric]

What we built:
→ [Solution component 1]
→ [Solution component 2]
→ [Solution component 3]

The results after [timeframe]:
✓ [Metric improvement 1]
✓ [Metric improvement 2]
✓ [Metric improvement 3]

Key insight: [One-line lesson]

Building something similar? Let's connect.
```

### Lesson Learned Post

```
I made a costly mistake on our last project.

Here's what happened:
[2-3 sentences of context]

What I learned:

1️⃣ [Lesson 1]
   → [Why it matters]

2️⃣ [Lesson 2]
   → [How to apply it]

3️⃣ [Lesson 3]
   → [What to avoid]

This cost us [consequence].
But it made us better.

Have you learned this the hard way too?
```

### Team/Hiring Post

```
We're looking for [role] who [unique characteristic].

Not just someone who can [basic skill].
Someone who [differentiating quality].

What you'll do:
• [Exciting responsibility 1]
• [Exciting responsibility 2]
• [Exciting responsibility 3]

What you'll get:
• [Benefit 1]
• [Benefit 2]
• [Benefit 3]

Know someone? Tag them 👇
Or DM me directly.
```

### Industry Insight Post

```
[Industry] is changing faster than most realize.

Here's what I'm seeing:

📊 Trend 1: [Observation + implication]

📊 Trend 2: [Observation + implication]

📊 Trend 3: [Observation + implication]

What does this mean for [audience]?

[Your take in 2-3 sentences]

What trends are you noticing?
```

## Power Words by Category

**Urgency**: Now, Today, Limited, Deadline, Instant, Fast
**Exclusivity**: Secret, Insider, Private, Hidden, Unlock
**Trust**: Proven, Guaranteed, Certified, Official, Verified
**Value**: Free, Bonus, Save, Discount, Exclusive
**Emotion**: Amazing, Incredible, Transform, Revolutionary
**Curiosity**: Discover, Revealed, Surprising, Little-known
**Action**: Get, Start, Join, Learn, Build, Create

## Hashtag Strategies by Platform

### LinkedIn

```
Mix broad + niche + branded
Example for software development:
#DigitalTransformation (broad)
#EnterpriseAI (medium)
#HRMSsolution (niche)
#KreupAI (branded)
#GCCTech (regional)
```

### Instagram

```
Layer 3 tiers + branded
Tier 1 (1M+): #Tech #Software
Tier 2 (100K-1M): #SaaS #StartupLife
Tier 3 (10K-100K): #ERPSoftware #FacilityTech
Branded: #YourCompanyName
```

### Twitter/X

```
Max 2-3, integrated naturally
"Building the future of HR automation #HRTech #AI"
```
