# Platform-Specific Guides

Detailed templates and best practices for each platform.

## LinkedIn

### Algorithm Insights (2024-2025)

- **Dwell time** is key: longer time spent = more distribution
- First hour engagement determines reach
- Comments > Reactions > Shares for algorithm
- Native content beats external links
- Carousel documents get 3x more engagement
- Video dwell time matters (hook in first 3 sec)

### Post Templates

**Thought Leadership**

```
[Bold statement or question that challenges conventional thinking]

Here's what most people get wrong about [topic]:

[Point 1 - with specific example]
[Point 2 - with unexpected insight]
[Point 3 - with actionable advice]

The truth?

[One-line insight that reframes the topic]

What's your take on [related question]?

#Hashtag1 #Hashtag2 #Hashtag3
```

**Project Announcement**

```
We just shipped [project name] for [client/industry].

The challenge: [specific problem in 1 line]

What we built:
→ [Feature 1 with benefit]
→ [Feature 2 with benefit]
→ [Feature 3 with benefit]

The result? [Quantifiable outcome]

Proud of the team for [specific achievement].

More details in comments 👇

#SoftwareDevelopment #[Industry] #[Tech]
```

**Story Post**

```
[Time reference] ago, [something happened].

[2-3 lines of context/setup]

Here's what I learned:

1. [Lesson with brief explanation]
2. [Lesson with brief explanation]
3. [Lesson with brief explanation]

[Reflection or broader implication]

Have you experienced something similar?

#Lesson #Growth #[Industry]
```

**Carousel Hook Slides**

```
Slide 1: [Bold claim] + "Swipe to learn →"
Slide 2: The problem / "Most people think..."
Slide 3-7: One point per slide, visual + text
Slide 8: Summary / Key takeaway
Slide 9: CTA + Profile mention
```

### LinkedIn Character Limits

- Post: 3,000 characters (aim for 1,500-2,500)
- Comment: 1,250 characters
- Headline: 220 characters
- About: 2,600 characters

## Facebook

### Algorithm Insights

- Meaningful interactions prioritized
- Groups and community content boosted
- Video (especially Live) gets reach
- Avoid engagement bait ("Like if you agree")
- Native uploads beat YouTube links

### Post Templates

**Company Update**

```
[Exciting news in 1 line] 🎉

[2-3 sentences with context and why it matters]

[CTA - tag people, ask question, or link]

#Hashtag
```

**Behind-the-Scenes**

```
A look inside [what we're working on]...

[Photo/Video + 2-3 casual sentences]

[Question to encourage comments]
```

**Event/Milestone**

```
[Celebration emoji] [What happened]

[Why it matters + thank you]

[What's next or CTA]
```

### Facebook Character Limits

- Post: 63,206 chars (aim for 100-250 for engagement)
- Page description: 255 characters

## Instagram

### Algorithm Insights

- Reels get 2x reach vs static posts
- Saves and shares > likes for ranking
- Hashtag research matters (use mix)
- Post at peak audience times
- Carousel posts get 3x engagement
- Stories with polls/questions boost engagement

### Post Templates

**Product/Project Carousel**

```
[Visually striking first image]

Caption:
[Hook line - benefit or curiosity]

[1-2 sentences of context]

[Bullet points or numbered list - brief]

[CTA: Save this, Share with someone, Link in bio]

.
.
.
[Hashtags in comment OR below line breaks]
```

**Reel Script Structure**

```
0-3 sec: HOOK (text on screen + verbal)
3-15 sec: CONTEXT (quick setup)
15-45 sec: VALUE (main content, keep pace fast)
45-60 sec: CTA (follow, save, comment)
```

**Story Sequence**

```
Story 1: Hook/Question (poll or quiz)
Story 2-4: Content/Answer
Story 5: CTA (swipe up, DM, link)
```

### Instagram Character Limits

- Caption: 2,200 characters (aim for 150-300)
- Bio: 150 characters
- Hashtags: 30 max (use 8-15 strategically)

### Hashtag Strategy

- 3-5 broad (1M+ posts): #DigitalMarketing
- 3-5 medium (100K-1M): #TechStartup
- 3-5 niche (10K-100K): #SaaSDevelopment
- 1-2 branded: #KreupAI

## Google Business Posts

### Post Types

- **What's New**: Updates, news, announcements
- **Events**: With date, time, CTA
- **Offers**: Promotions with terms
- **Products**: Direct product highlights

### Templates

**What's New**

```
[Headline: 5-7 words, action-oriented]

[2-3 sentences: What, Why it matters, CTA]

[Button: Learn More / Call Now / Book]
```

**Event Post**

```
[Event Name]

📅 [Date]
🕐 [Time]
📍 [Location/Online]

[1-2 sentences: What attendees get]

[Button: Sign Up / Learn More]
```

**Offer Post**

```
[Offer headline: X% Off / Free Y]

[Terms: Valid until, conditions]

[How to redeem]

[Button: Get Offer / Book Now]
```

### Google Posts Best Practices

- Keep under 300 characters (150-200 optimal)
- Include high-quality image (400x300px min)
- Use clear CTA buttons
- Post weekly minimum
- No hashtags needed
- Expires after 7 days (events after event date)

## Blog Posts

### SEO Structure

**Title Formula**: [Number/Power Word] + [Keyword] + [Benefit/Promise]

- Examples:
  - "7 ERP Implementation Mistakes That Cost Companies Millions"
  - "The Complete Guide to Digital Transformation in Manufacturing"
  - "How We Built a Scalable HRMS Platform in 6 Months"

**Meta Description**: 150-160 chars, includes primary keyword + compelling hook

### Blog Template

```markdown
# [SEO-Optimized Title]

[Hook paragraph - story, statistic, or bold claim]

[Promise paragraph - what reader will learn]

---

## [H2: First Major Point]

[2-3 paragraphs with examples]

### [H3: Sub-point if needed]

[Content with bullet points or numbered lists]

## [H2: Second Major Point]

[Content...]

## [H2: Continue pattern...]

---

## Key Takeaways

- [Takeaway 1]
- [Takeaway 2]
- [Takeaway 3]

## Next Steps / CTA

[What should reader do now? Contact, download, subscribe...]

---

_[Author bio with link]_

**Related Posts:**

- [Related post 1]
- [Related post 2]
```

### Blog Best Practices

- 1,500-2,500 words for SEO
- Include images every 300-500 words
- Use internal and external links
- Optimize for featured snippets (use questions as H2s)
- Include schema markup suggestions
- Write for humans first, SEO second
